# 0011. Sound, music and haptics

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-07
- Owner: Nicolás Hermosilla P., agent
- Related ADRs: 0031

## Goal
The game makes sound: effects for the events of doc 15, a looping soundtrack in the menus and races, and
vibration on devices that have it, each with its own on/off setting. The user asked for sound next; the
agent accepted this intent, its ADR and slices on that instruction, and the user reviews the result. The
RG351P benchmark stays skipped at the user's request. Nothing from the original is used: the sounds and the
music are our own, synthesised by a script in the repository.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Effects** (doc 15): a click when a menu item is activated; a beep for each start light and a start sound
  when racing begins; shield gained (a spot or the recharger); an obstacle smashed or destroyed by a torpedo;
  the zone countdown starting; a bonus picked up; Quake fired; plasma touched; a torpedo launched. Added by us:
  crash, race finished, Phase Shift and bonus fired sounds. Each plays once per event, at 0.5 to 1.0 volume.
- **Music**: one looping track plays in the menus and during runs, started when the game opens, continuing
  through pause and screens, with its own volume.
- **Settings**: the Settings page has `sound`, `music` and `vibration` items (on or off), saved on the device.
  With sound off no effect plays; with music off the track is stopped; with vibration off nothing vibrates.
- **Vibration** (doc 15) on devices that have it: obstacle smashed, torpedo fired and zone countdown 50 ms;
  shield gained and plasma touched 100 ms; recharger and Phase Shift 150 ms; bonus picked up 200 ms; Quake
  300 ms; crash 500 ms.
- **Assets**: generated by `tools/make-audio.py` into `assets/audio/` (WAV, mono, 22.05 kHz), committed, and
  shipped in every package; no third-party audio.
- Silent where there is no audio device: the game runs and the tests pass with the dummy audio driver.
- The existing behaviour is unchanged.
- The user reviews it by ear.

## Out of scope
- Separate volume sliders, positional or doppler effects, the Space Fold and Time Dilation "loaded" sounds the
  original rarely plays, per-screen music, a licence-free recording of real instruments.

## Open questions
- None; the sounds are first versions to be refined by ear.
